# MiA! — Emote Art Brief (Clash Royale style)

> **Status:** the shipping art is vector, generated by `scripts/emote-art/generate.mjs`
> (42 PNGs — 21 expressions x 2 characters). This brief is the upgrade path to painted
> Supercell-style art. The character designs below are the same ones the generator draws,
> so replacement art stays on-model; ingest it with `VOICE=<female|male> scripts/emote-art.sh`
> and no app code changes.

Production brief for generating the emote images defined in [emote-spec.md](emote-spec.md) —
**21 expressions per character, for both Ace (female) and Deuce (male)**.
Written for an AI image generator with reference-image support (Nano Banana / Gemini
image, GPT-image, or Midjourney with `--cref`). Everything needed is here.

**The one rule that matters:** generate the character sheet in step 1, then feed that
image back as a reference for all 21 emotes. Prompt-only generation will drift — you'll
get 21 different raccoons. The workflow below exists to prevent that.

---

## 1. Hard constraints

| | |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1024×1024 square, generated; delivered at **512×512** |
| Background | **Transparent PNG.** Generate on flat `#00FF00` pure green if the model can't do alpha, then key it out (script in §6) |
| Text | **None.** No words, no speech bubbles, no phrase baked in — the app draws the phrase |
| Framing | Bust: head + shoulders + one hand. Not full body — this is shown at **64px** |
| Readability | Must read at 64px. Silhouette test in §7 is not optional |
| Consistency | Same character, same colors, same outline weight across all 21 |

## 2. The mascots: **Deuce** (male) and **Ace** (female)

A pair of sly raccoon card-sharps — raccoons already read as *masked bandit*, which is
exactly the tell for a bluffing game. Each player's emotes use the mascot matching their
assigned voice, so both need the full 21 expressions.

**The pair differ by** (all four must read at 64px): Deuce wears a **teal** visor over a
**magenta** waistcoat, blunt ears, heavier brows, wider muzzle. Ace inverts the accents —
**magenta** visor over a **teal** waistcoat — plus tall tufted ears, eyelashes, a gold
hoop earring, and a slimmer muzzle. Everything else below is shared.

**Species & build.** Anthropomorphic raccoon, chunky caricature proportions: head is
**40% of total height**, huge expressive hands (4 fingers), narrow shoulders, barrel chest.
Think Clash Royale's Bandit or Goblin — oversized head, tiny everything else.

**Face.** Broad muzzle, wide black bandit mask across the eyes, cream-white brow patches
above the mask, black button nose, one **gold tooth** in a lopsided grin. Eyes are large
ovals with big pupils and a hard white specular dot. Eyebrows sit *above* the mask and do
the heavy lifting on every expression.

**Fur.** Warm mid-grey body (`#8A8280`), cream chest/muzzle (`#F7F3EC`), near-black mask
and ears (`#232222`), ringed tail (grey/near-black bands) — tail visible only when the pose
allows it.

**Wardrobe (locked — never varies).**
- **Teal dealer's visor** (`#1DB6BB`) with a darker teal underbrim, worn slightly crooked
- **Magenta waistcoat** (`#DE1A62`) with a popped collar and two amber buttons (`#F59F0C`)
- Rolled shirtsleeves in cream, one **amber die cufflink**
- Never a hat, never a hoodie, never sunglasses

**Signature prop.** A single **cream d6 with amber pips** is present in *every* emote — in
a hand, pinched in fingers, balanced on a shoulder, mid-air, or bitten. It's the second
silhouette anchor after the visor, and it's the thing that makes the set feel like a set.

### The four consistency anchors
Every image must contain these, unmistakable at 64px. Check each one before accepting an image:
1. **Teal visor** (crooked, on top of the head)
2. **Black bandit mask** across the eyes
3. **Magenta waistcoat** with amber buttons
4. **One cream die** somewhere in frame

## 3. Style recipe — the Supercell look

Clash Royale's look is not "flat vector." It is painted, chunky, and lit. Six things do it:

1. **Thick keyline.** A uniform dark outline around the whole silhouette and major interior
   shapes — **6–8px at 1024px**, tapering slightly at fine details. Color is a dark warm
   plum-brown (`#2B1B22`), *never* pure black.
2. **Cel shading + soft blend.** 2–3 value steps per material with a soft airbrushed
   transition between them. Not a hard 2-tone cel, not a smooth gradient — in between.
3. **Warm key, cool bounce.** Key light from **upper left**, warm and bright. Cool
   blue-violet bounce from lower right. A crisp **rim light along the upper-left silhouette**
   is what makes it pop off a dark background.
4. **Gloss.** Hard white specular dot in each eye, a highlight on the gold tooth, a sheen
   band on the visor brim, subtle satin sheen on the waistcoat.
5. **Saturation + value contrast.** Fully saturated brand hues against dark fur. Squint —
   the head should separate from the body, and the die should separate from the hand.
6. **Slight low camera.** Viewed a touch from below in a **3/4 view** — gives the character
   presence instead of looking like a sticker.

**Exaggeration is the point.** These are 64px expressions. Push each one two steps past
where it feels natural: eyebrows into the visor, mouth to a third of the face, pupils
shrunk to dots or blown to saucers. A subtle expression is a failed expression here.

## 4. Workflow

### Step 1 — Generate the character sheet (do this once, get it right)

```
Character design sheet for a mobile game mascot: "Deuce", an anthropomorphic
raccoon card-sharp. Chunky caricature proportions, head is 40% of total height,
oversized expressive four-fingered hands, barrel chest, narrow shoulders.
Broad muzzle, wide black bandit mask across the eyes, cream-white brow patches
above the mask, black button nose, lopsided grin with one gold tooth, large oval
eyes with big pupils and hard white specular highlights, expressive eyebrows
above the mask. Warm mid-grey fur, cream chest and muzzle, near-black mask and
ears, ringed tail. Wearing a crooked teal dealer's visor, a magenta waistcoat
with a popped collar and two amber buttons, rolled cream shirtsleeves, one amber
die cufflink. Holding a single cream six-sided die with amber pips.

Style: Clash Royale / Supercell mobile game art. Thick dark plum-brown keyline
around the silhouette, cel shading with two to three value steps and soft
airbrushed transitions, warm key light from upper left, cool blue-violet bounce
from lower right, crisp rim light on the upper-left edge, glossy specular
highlights. Saturated, high contrast, painted 3D-cartoon feel. Confident, sly,
a little shady — belongs at a back-room card table.

Sheet layout: front view, 3/4 view, and side view of the head and torso, plus
four expression studies (sly grin, outraged shout, mock sobbing, bored yawn).
Flat pure green background (#00FF00). No text, no labels, no watermarks.
```

Regenerate until you have a Deuce you actually like — **every one of the 21 emotes
inherits this design**, so a mediocre sheet costs you the whole set. Save it as
`docs/art-ref/deuce-sheet.png`.

### Step 2 — Generate each emote against the reference

For each of the 21 rows in §5:

1. Attach `deuce-sheet.png` as the reference image (Nano Banana/GPT-image: "use this
   character"; Midjourney: `--cref <url> --cw 100`)
2. Prompt = **BASE BLOCK** + the row's **Pose** and **Face** text
3. Generate 4 variants, keep the one that passes §7

**BASE BLOCK** — prepend to every emote prompt:

```
The same character from the reference image — Deuce, the raccoon card-sharp in a
crooked teal dealer's visor and magenta waistcoat with amber buttons, black
bandit mask, one gold tooth. Identical design, colors, and proportions to the
reference.

Bust framing: head, shoulders, and one hand only, centered, filling the frame.
3/4 view from a slightly low angle. A single cream die with amber pips visible.

Style: Clash Royale / Supercell mobile game art. Thick dark plum-brown keyline,
cel shading with soft airbrushed transitions, warm key light upper left, cool
bounce lower right, crisp rim light on the upper-left silhouette, glossy
speculars in the eyes. Saturated and high contrast. Exaggerated cartoon
expression, readable as a 64-pixel icon.

Flat pure green background (#00FF00), nothing else in frame. No text, no letters,
no speech bubble, no border, no watermark.
```

### Step 3 — Post-process
Run `scripts/emote-art.sh` (§6). Keys the green, trims, centers, resizes to 512×512
transparent PNG, names the file by emote id.

### Step 4 — QA
The 64px contact sheet from the same script. Squint test in §7.

## 6. Post-processing

`scripts/emote-art.sh` handles the whole ingest — it needs only `ffmpeg`, which is already
on this machine.

```bash
# One file: key the green, trim, center, resize to 512x512, save as <id>.png
scripts/emote-art.sh raw/objection-v3.png objection

# Whole folder: any file named <id>.* or <id>-<anything>.* gets processed
scripts/emote-art.sh --batch raw/

# 64px contact sheet of everything in assets/emotes/ — the QA artifact
scripts/emote-art.sh --sheet
```

If the model gives you true alpha already, the script detects it and skips the key step.

---

## 7. QA — the squint test

An emote that fails any of these gets regenerated, not shipped.

- [ ] **64px squint test.** Open the contact sheet, lean back, squint. Can you name the
      emotion without reading the label? If not, the expression isn't pushed hard enough.
- [ ] **All four anchors present** (visor, mask, waistcoat, die) and each recognizable at 64px
- [ ] **Silhouette holds.** Head separates from body; the gesture hand doesn't merge into the torso
- [ ] **No text anywhere** — models love sneaking in letters, sound effects, and signatures
- [ ] **Clean alpha.** No green fringe, no white halo, no leftover background rectangle
- [ ] **Consistent character.** Put it next to the other 20: same fur value, same visor teal,
      same outline weight, same eye size. Drift usually shows first in muzzle length and visor angle
- [ ] **Centered with breathing room.** Character fills ~85% of the canvas, not touching the edges

## 8. Negative prompt

Append to any generator that supports it:

```
text, letters, words, numbers, watermark, signature, logo, speech bubble, caption,
border, frame, drop shadow on background, full body, feet, multiple characters,
photorealistic, 3D render, anime, chibi eyes without mask, flat vector, thin lines,
muted colors, pastel, busy background, gradient background, cluttered detail
```

Common failure modes and the fix:
- **Character drifts** → reference weight too low; raise `--cw`, or re-attach the sheet
- **Full body / tiny head** → repeat "bust framing, head and shoulders only, fills the frame"
- **Text sneaks in** → the phrase words are leaking; drop the phrase from the prompt entirely
- **Too subtle** → add "extremely exaggerated, pushed to the maximum, cartoon extreme"
- **Green fringe after keying** → regenerate on green with no green in the character, or
  raise `similarity` in the script's colorkey
